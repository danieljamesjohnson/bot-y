<objective>
Assert the two criteria that are about the schedule's shape rather than about its size, on top of the
mechanism 09-02 landed — and record what the shorter tick costs the daemon, in numbers, before 09-04
has to price it.

**Purpose.** 09-02 owns the mechanism and every red the mechanism created; it hands wave 3 a green
tree. What it deliberately does not carry is the pair of criteria that are pure additions:
criterion 1's **stated separation in seconds** and criterion 2's **independence in both directions**.
They are new tests over working code, they need no production edit, and putting them in 09-02 would
have pushed that plan past its context budget on top of a ten-test repair sweep. Splitting them here
is a scheduling decision, not a scope reduction: the criteria are asserted in full, in this wave, on
the same files.

**Two things this plan must not do, and both have a named failure behind them.** Criterion 1 says the
bound is asserted *"on the schedule, never on a wall clock"* — a scheduler test that measures elapsed
time passes on a fast host and flakes on a loaded one, and this repository has been bitten by a gate
whose failure could not be attributed. And criterion 2 says *"in both directions"*, which means the
negative half is asserted explicitly: `changing X moved X` says nothing about whether Y moved with it.

**And one thing it must do that the criteria do not ask for.** Most of `tests/test_pacing.py` builds
a `Pacer` with no roster and no tick, which defaults the tolerance and the birth phase back to
today's behaviour. `cli.watch_loop` always passes both. So these criteria are asserted under the
**shipping** construction as well, or they would be true of a `Pacer` nobody runs.

**Output.** Criterion 1's separation as a written-out literal, criterion 2's independence in both
directions with explicit negative halves, proof the loop sleeps the tick, both clock terms observed
defended, and the daemon's new wake and write rates written down as numbers.

## The five criteria this phase is judged on (verbatim from ROADMAP.md, unedited)

  1. Two retailers whose intervals coincide are **not** dispatched inside the same short window; the bound is a stated number of seconds and is asserted on the schedule, never on a wall clock
  2. Each retailer's next-attempt time is **independent**: changing one retailer's interval or backoff moves that retailer's schedule and no other's, asserted in both directions
  3. Over a simulated day, the maximum number of retailers requested within any 60-second window is a **stated number**, and it is smaller than the current six
  4. **No regression in what already works**: per-retailer cadence and backoff still hold, and `boty check` — a deliberate full pass, which is a different thing from the daemon's schedule — still completes inside REQ-08's 2-minute budget, re-measured rather than assumed
  5. `make verify-offline` exits 0, with at least one new mutation registered and observed CAUGHT

**This plan owns criteria 1 and 2 and nothing else.** Criterion 3 was discharged in 09-02; criterion
4 is 09-04's and 09-05's; criterion 5 is 09-05's. This plan runs `make verify-offline` as a
regression check and says so.
</objective>
