<objective>
Take the six retailers out of lockstep — a deterministic per-retailer **phase**, a loop **tick**
shorter than the shortest standing cadence, and a `record` that advances on the retailer's own grid
instead of re-anchoring every retailer that fires together to the same clock — **and bring the tree
back green in the same plan.**

**Purpose.** REQ-23 says the fleet must stop presenting as one coordinated crawler. The lockstep is
structural, and `09-DECISIONS.md` § *Collision 1* proves no offset alone can fix it: four retailers
are configured at the 300 s default, the loop makes requests only at a tick, and a 300 s tick offers
exactly one tick per 300 s span — so all four must be asked at that one tick whatever phase they are
given. The tick has to shrink, and the schedule under it has to hold a phase.

**Two terms, and the second is the one a reader will try to drop.** A birth phase alone collapses:
`record` currently re-anchors every retailer to the cycle's `now`, so two retailers that fire
together are in lockstep from then on, and under the loop's jitter a retailer that drifts into
another's slot stays there — the merge is absorbing. So `record` must advance from the retailer's own
previous due time.

**And that second term is UNCONDITIONAL, which is what makes this plan bigger than it looks.** The
roster and tick defaults protect the tolerance and the birth phase on a `Pacer` built without them —
that is what keeps most of the suite's construction sites meaningful — but **`record`'s advance is
not defaulted away by anything.** It reddens ten existing tests, listed by name in Task 3. Six of
them live in `tests/test_pacing.py`, **which no plan after this one owns**, so leaving them for a
later wave is not a bad habit here, it is impossible. They are repaired in the plan that breaks them.

**The alternative was considered and refused.** Gating the grid advance behind a non-empty roster
would keep every old test green with no edits at all. It is rejected: `boty/cli.py`'s `watch_loop`
always passes a roster, so every defaulted site — most of the suite — would then exercise a code path
the daemon never takes. That buys a small diff by making the evidence describe something that does
not ship, and the evidence is the only thing this phase produces.

**What this plan does NOT touch, and it is the load-bearing half.** `Pacer.current_interval` comes
out byte-identical, proved against a recorded digest. Phase 7 made it the single expression behind
both the fetch schedule and the published `current_interval_seconds`; Phase 8 put the cool-off inside
its `max()` rather than beside it and argues that at three sites. A phase is a **position**, not a
duration: a retailer at a 300 s cadence with a phase of 150 s is still asked every 300 s, and still
publishes 300.

**Output.** A fleet whose retailers no longer coincide, criterion 3's after-number transcribed from a
run and strictly smaller than 09-01's six, ten enumerated tests closed with their withdrawn claims
quoted, and `make verify-offline` green.

## The five criteria this phase is judged on (verbatim from ROADMAP.md, unedited)

  1. Two retailers whose intervals coincide are **not** dispatched inside the same short window; the bound is a stated number of seconds and is asserted on the schedule, never on a wall clock
  2. Each retailer's next-attempt time is **independent**: changing one retailer's interval or backoff moves that retailer's schedule and no other's, asserted in both directions
  3. Over a simulated day, the maximum number of retailers requested within any 60-second window is a **stated number**, and it is smaller than the current six
  4. **No regression in what already works**: per-retailer cadence and backoff still hold, and `boty check` — a deliberate full pass, which is a different thing from the daemon's schedule — still completes inside REQ-08's 2-minute budget, re-measured rather than assumed
  5. `make verify-offline` exits 0, with at least one new mutation registered and observed CAUGHT

**This plan owns the after-half of criterion 3.** Criteria 1 and 2 are asserted in **09-03**, on top
of the mechanism this plan lands — they are additive tests over a green tree, which is why they are
their own wave rather than a fourth task here. Criterion 4 is 09-04's and 09-05's; criterion 5 is
09-05's. This plan runs `make verify-offline` as a regression check and says so.
</objective>
