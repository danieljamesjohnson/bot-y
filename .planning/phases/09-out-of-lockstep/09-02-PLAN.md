<objective>
Take the six retailers out of lockstep: a deterministic per-retailer **phase**, a loop **tick**
shorter than the shortest standing cadence, and a `record` that advances on the retailer's own grid
instead of re-anchoring every retailer that fires together to the same clock.

**Purpose.** REQ-23 says the fleet must stop presenting as one coordinated crawler. The lockstep is
structural, and `09-DECISIONS.md` § *Collision 1* proves that no offset alone can fix it: four
retailers are configured at the 300 s default, the loop makes requests only at a tick, and a 300 s
tick offers exactly one tick per 300 s span — so all four must be asked at that one tick whatever
phase they are given. The tick has to shrink, and the schedule under it has to hold a phase.

**Two terms, and the second is the one a reader will try to drop.** A birth phase alone collapses:
`record` currently re-anchors every retailer to the cycle's `now`, so two retailers that fire
together are in lockstep from then on, and under the loop's jitter a retailer that drifts into
another's slot stays there — the merge is absorbing. So `record` must advance from the retailer's own
previous due time. `09-DECISIONS.md` § *Collision 3* records this, and Task 1's tracer is what proves
the two terms compose before anything is generalised.

**What this plan does NOT touch, and it is the load-bearing half.** `Pacer.current_interval` comes
out of this plan byte-identical. Phase 7 made it the single expression behind both the fetch schedule
and the published `current_interval_seconds`; Phase 8 put the cool-off inside its `max()` rather than
beside it and argues that at three sites. A phase is a **position**, not a duration: a retailer at a
300 s cadence with a phase of 150 s is still asked every 300 s, and still publishes 300.

**Output.** A fleet whose retailers no longer coincide, with criterion 1's separation stated in
seconds, criterion 2's independence asserted in both directions, and criterion 3's after-number
transcribed from a run and strictly smaller than 09-01's six.

## The five criteria this phase is judged on (verbatim from ROADMAP.md, unedited)

  1. Two retailers whose intervals coincide are **not** dispatched inside the same short window; the bound is a stated number of seconds and is asserted on the schedule, never on a wall clock
  2. Each retailer's next-attempt time is **independent**: changing one retailer's interval or backoff moves that retailer's schedule and no other's, asserted in both directions
  3. Over a simulated day, the maximum number of retailers requested within any 60-second window is a **stated number**, and it is smaller than the current six
  4. **No regression in what already works**: per-retailer cadence and backoff still hold, and `boty check` — a deliberate full pass, which is a different thing from the daemon's schedule — still completes inside REQ-08's 2-minute budget, re-measured rather than assumed
  5. `make verify-offline` exits 0, with at least one new mutation registered and observed CAUGHT

**This plan owns criteria 1, 2 and the after-half of 3.** Criterion 4 is 09-03's and 09-04's;
criterion 5 is 09-04's. This plan runs `make verify-offline` as a regression check and says so.
</objective>
