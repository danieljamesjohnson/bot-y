<objective>
Pay the price of a shorter tick out loud, and prove that nothing which already worked stopped
working.

**Purpose.** 09-02 made the loop wake several times per standing cadence. That buys criterion 1 and
criterion 3, and it changes three things nobody asked it to change. The largest is that **most ticks
now ask nobody**, and `boty/status.py` computes its aggregate health verdict as `all(...)` over the
health entries a pass produced — which is `True` over an empty list. Today an empty pass is only
reachable when every retailer is backed off; after 09-02 it is routine. A monitor publishing *nothing
went wrong* on a pass that asked nothing is a green dashboard over a question nobody asked, which is
the defect this entire project exists one level up to prevent. Rebuilding it inside the fix for a
different one would be the worst available way to fail this phase.

The other two are smaller and neither is free: two failure counters were sized against a 300-second
cycle and one of them **sends a notification**, and `boty/config.py` argues a live validation rule
from a sentence that went false.

**And then criterion 4's first half.** *"Per-retailer cadence and backoff still hold"* is a claim
about Phase 8's BEHAVIOURS surviving Phase 9's change, and it is asserted here rather than inferred
from a green suite. Two things make that harder than it sounds, and both are stated up front rather
than discovered: several of Phase 8's tests were **adjusted in 09-02** because `record`'s advance
changed unconditionally, so "unchanged" is the wrong question and *"does the same behaviour still
hold, and where did its expression have to move"* is the right one; and most of those tests build a
`Pacer` with **no roster and no tick**, which is not the shape `cli.watch_loop` ships, so a sweep run
only on them cannot fail for the reason it exists.

**Output.** An honest status document on an idle tick, two counters whose wall-clock meaning is
stated, one dated reversal, and a recorded regression sweep with numbers in it.

## The five criteria this phase is judged on (verbatim from ROADMAP.md, unedited)

  1. Two retailers whose intervals coincide are **not** dispatched inside the same short window; the bound is a stated number of seconds and is asserted on the schedule, never on a wall clock
  2. Each retailer's next-attempt time is **independent**: changing one retailer's interval or backoff moves that retailer's schedule and no other's, asserted in both directions
  3. Over a simulated day, the maximum number of retailers requested within any 60-second window is a **stated number**, and it is smaller than the current six
  4. **No regression in what already works**: per-retailer cadence and backoff still hold, and `boty check` — a deliberate full pass, which is a different thing from the daemon's schedule — still completes inside REQ-08's 2-minute budget, re-measured rather than assumed
  5. `make verify-offline` exits 0, with at least one new mutation registered and observed CAUGHT

**This plan owns criterion 4's FIRST half — cadence and backoff — and nothing else.** The `boty
check` budget is 09-05's, and 09-05 states it as MET IN PART if that is what it is. Criterion 5 is
09-05's. This plan runs `make verify-offline` as a regression check and says so.
</objective>
