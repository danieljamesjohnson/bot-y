Settle the collisions in writing, then wire one path end to end: a Best Buy control whose SKU no
longer resolves is reported as a **dead control** — a fact about our configuration — instead of the
broken detector it is reported as today. Driven entirely from a fixture, with no live read.

**Purpose.** Criterion 1 says plainly that *today the first is reported as the third*. The defect is
one arm and one predicate away from being right, and everything needed to fix it already exists in
the tree — the branch that notices an unresolved SKU, the parse result that says whether the markup
was readable, the retailer's own canonical link, and two captures that disagree in exactly the way
the question asks. What is missing is a **fact** rather than a sentence:
`retailers._verdict_from_html` writes the diagnosis into `Result.detail`, and
`monitor.assess_health` is forbidden from reading prose.

**The predicate is not obvious and both easy readings are wrong.** Measured at planning time over
both Best Buy captures: the true-dead page carries **zero** JSON-LD blocks, so
`unparseable == 0` alone would mark a reskin as a dead control (`T-10-01`, rated high), and
`blocks > 0 and unparseable == 0` would make the dead state unreachable from that capture. The
outline's § *The predicate, stated exactly* carries the measured table and the disjunction this plan
ships. **Do not re-derive it; do not simplify it.**

**And two things break the moment the fifth arm exists**, both repaired in this wave because both are
this plan's own collateral: M30's mutation anchor is the action line this plan rewrites, and
`tests/test_alert_text.py`'s two partitions enumerate four arms by name and go false without going
red.

**This closes Definition of Done item 3's second half** — *"the dead-control state is reachable in a
test without one"* (a real reading). The other half needs the wire and is `10-04`'s.

**Output.** `10-DECISIONS.md`; a field on `Result` and a field on `Health`; a discriminated producer;
a dead-control arm in `assess_health`; and one end-to-end test that starts at a captured page and
ends at a `Health` that names our own config file.
