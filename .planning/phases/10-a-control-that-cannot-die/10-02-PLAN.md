<objective>
Finish criterion 1 by giving the dead-control state its second producer, and close criterion 2 —
which is a conjunction, and whose second half is a real defect in the shipped code rather than a
theoretical one.

**Purpose.** `10-01` proved the shape on one path: Best Buy, addressed by SKU, dead by
non-resolution. Every other retailer here is addressed by URL, and a dead target there is an **HTTP
404** — which `fetch.get` raises with its status attached and which `is_refusal` correctly declines
to treat as a refusal. So it lands in the breakage arm and is reported as a probably-broken detector.
That is the identical defect criterion 1 names, at five retailers rather than one, and it is one
mirror function away from being right.

**Criterion 2's second half is where the work is.** A group holding one dead control and one refused
control satisfies neither `all` today, falls through to the breakage arm, and is described with a
sentence asserting that nothing was refused — about a group containing a refusal. That is a real
refusal silenced by a dead control, which is precisely what the criterion forbids.

**Output.** A status-level producer, wired at every arm that already asks the refusal question; the
rung-3 gap named rather than assumed closed; and criterion 2's two halves asserted separately.
</objective>

forgets the other reddens rather than producing a status that is both a refusal and a resolution
failure.

**Test the whole boundary, not the happy case:** 404 and 410 true; 401, 403 and 429 false and still
refusals; 500 and 502 false and not refusals; a transport error with no status false. And the
disjointness assertion over both sets.
