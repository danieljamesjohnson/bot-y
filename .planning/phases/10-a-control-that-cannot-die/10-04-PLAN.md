<objective>
Spend the three reads Dan authorised, and let Best Buy's answer — whatever it is — be the
measurement.

**Purpose.** Criterion 3 and Definition of Done item 3's first half both require a **real** reading:
*repaired — measured against a real reading, not a fixture — or replaced with one that reads, with
the replacement's durability argued.* This is the only plan in the phase that touches the wire, and
it is fourth on purpose: criteria 1, 2, 4 and 5 are closed offline before the first request, so a
refusal cannot strand the phase.

**What has not been measured, and is the reason read 1 exists.** REQ-24 says *Best Buy's current dead
control is repaired*. The last recorded live reading of that control says `fetch failed: no
Chrome/Chromium binary found`. **That is a local capability failure — not a dead control, not a
refusal, and not a reading.** `10-CONTEXT.md` records the capability claim as stale, measured
2026-09-02. So the incumbent may be alive, dead, refused, or unreadable here, and which one is
**not established**. Read 1 establishes it, and if it turns out the control was never dead but only
unread, that correction is a better outcome than a repair.

**Output.** Three reads or fewer, spaced and recorded; a control changed only if a replacement was
confirmed; and criterion 3's verdict stated under a branch table written before the first request.
</objective>
