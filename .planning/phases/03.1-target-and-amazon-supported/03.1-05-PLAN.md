<objective>
Add the second axis, before the retailer that needs it.

03.1-02 established by measurement that Target serves its product page and
withholds the product data: HTTP 200, ~315 KB, no challenge, `"isBot": false`,
and zero `ld+json`, zero `"price"`, zero `availability`, zero `"seller"`. The
button, however, renders. Dan's decision is that reading it — the method his
original pre-bot-y script used on GameStop and Walmart — is supported.

**How that is represented is the whole of this plan, and Dan chose the shape: a
second axis, not a new rung.**

- `Rung` keeps meaning **transport** — TLS, API, BROWSER, and rung 4 stays
  "dropped, with the evidence written down". **Nothing is renumbered.**
- `Extraction` is added — `structured` or `dom`.
- `Result.degraded` widens to fire on **either** a browser transport **or** a dom
  extraction.

The distinction being captured is real and today's model cannot express it. Best
Buy is rung 3 + **structured**: a browser renders the page, and what is read off
it is the retailer's own `ld+json` — a machine-readable feed they maintain
because Google Shopping depends on it. Target would be rung 3 + **dom**:
presentation markup, which a reskin breaks silently, which is the exact failure
mode this project exists to catch. Both are "browser". Lumping them together
hides a real difference in what a reading is worth.

Widening `degraded` is not cosmetic. It is derived from the rung alone today, so
a rung-1 DOM adapter — cheap to write, and the most fragile thing anyone could
add to this codebase — would ship looking fully trustworthy on the dashboard, in
`boty check`, and in the support matrix. That is a latent hole, and this plan
closes it before the first DOM adapter exists rather than after.

Purpose: shared infrastructure for the extraction axis, in the role 02-02 played
for DEGRADED — consumed by the matrix, by Target, and by anything Amazon needs.
Output: the `Extraction` enum, a widened `degraded` with mutation coverage for
both of its disjuncts, and a matrix column with two-directional rules.
</objective>

- **Deliberately NOT a member of `Availability`.** `cli.SYMBOL` is indexed
  unconditionally by `r.availability`, and `monitor.assess_health` and
