## Accomplishments

- Closed a latent hole before the adapter that would have exposed it: `degraded` was derived from the rung alone, so a rung-1 DOM adapter — cheap to write, and the most fragile thing this codebase could acquire — would have shipped looking fully trustworthy in `boty check`, on the status page and in the support matrix. Target would be rung 3 + `dom` (presentation markup, which a reskin breaks silently). Both are "browser"; the model can now tell them apart.
- Gave the new disjunct its own mutation. **7/7 mutations caught**, M7 included, killed by 4 tests.
- Widened the matrix half of criterion 3 in the same commit as the runtime half, so the two cannot drift.
- `make verify` run under the service `EnvironmentFile` — bare `VERIFY: PASS`, 4/4 **live** controls including Best Buy's browser rung, not an `INCOMPLETE` green.

## Decisions Made

- **The axis is added, not the ladder extended.** `Rung` keeps meaning transport. Folding extraction into it would have renumbered a scale four phases of documents refer to by number, and made rung 4's meaning ("dropped, with the evidence written down") say something it does not.
- **`Extraction` is a member of neither `Availability` nor `Rung`, and is not fed into `Health`.** All three claims are written into the enum's docstring, because each is a trap: a fourth `Availability` member is a `KeyError` in `cli.SYMBOL` mid-report, and a dom reading that flipped `Health.ok` would raise a permanent health warning that never clears, making the "no health warnings" criterion unreachable by construction.
- **One flag, two disjuncts, two mutations.** `degraded` answers one question. `rung` and `extraction` are both published so a reader can tell which reason fired — a browser is slow, a DOM read rots silently, and those are different things to plan around. M6 proves the flag exists; only M7 proves the new half is load-bearing.
- **The dashboard was extended, not just the tests.** The `degraded` tooltip literally read "read through a browser (rung 3)", which the widening falsified. A dom reading is now visible on the page rather than only counted.
