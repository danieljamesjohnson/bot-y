# 11-01 — The measurement, and the three verdicts it supports

**Phase:** 11 — The Honest Ladder Position · **Criteria:** 1, 2, 4 (partly) · **Wave:** 1 of 2

**Live requests this plan may make: ZERO.** See `COVERAGE.md`. Reading `journalctl -u boty`,
`state.json` and `served/boty/status.json` is permitted; writing any of the daemon's three
documents is forbidden by `CLAUDE.md` and nothing here needs to.

---

## What this plan establishes

A dated, cited verdict in `docs/retailer-evidence.md` for **each** of Amazon, Target and Walmart,
stating rung, extraction axis, what was tried, and what the last three days of the daemon's own
readings actually show.

## The measurement window, fixed before the numbers are taken

`2026-09-11 07:56:33 CDT → 2026-09-14 08:18:35 CDT`, **3 d 0 h 22 m**, one daemon
(`MainPID=746345`, `NRestarts=0`, `ActiveState=active`). The lower bound is the restart that put
phases 8, 9 and 10 on the wire; nothing before it is the same code and nothing before it is quoted
as if it were.

## Tasks

1. **Fix the counting rule before counting** — write down, in the record, what each source can and
   cannot support. Specifically: `monitor.State.record` returns early on `UNKNOWN`, so a `read_at`
   stamp is written **only** for a RESOLVED reading and a refusal can never write one. That single
   fact is what turns `read_at: None` from "we do not know" into "this retailer has produced no
   resolved reading". **Assert it from the code, at a line number, not from memory.**

2. **Extract the per-retailer series** from the journal: every `<retailer> refused us (N in a row)`
   with its timestamp, for all six retailers and not only the three — because a claim about three
   retailers needs the other three as the contrast that makes it mean anything.

3. **Write the three verdicts** into `docs/retailer-evidence.md`, one dated subsection each, under
   the existing `## Amazon`, `## Target` and `## Walmart` sections. **Never a new `## ` heading** —
   `scripts/evidence_check.py` splits on `## ` and asks its questions per retailer section, and a
   fourth heading for a retailer that exists would break the one-section-per-retailer rule.

4. **Decide each rung honestly, and record the reasoning where it is not obvious.** The expected
   landing, from the context's measurements:
   - **Target** — at rung 3 and refused there; `px-captcha`, 2026-08-27. No resolved reading in the
     window. In cool-off since 2026-09-14 02:00:00. The ladder above it is empty.
   - **Walmart** — rung 1 refused AND rung 3 refused (2026-08-27 spike, 3.8 s to a challenge page).
     No resolved reading in the window. The ladder above it is empty.
   - **Amazon** — rung 1, `dom`, **and it reads**. Two resolved stamps on 2026-09-14 and a refusal
     counter that reset. **It is not rung 4 and must not be recorded as one.**

5. **Answer criterion 2 explicitly, in writing, as UNANSWERED** — naming what was tried and what
   could not be. The decisive test is the same three requests from a second egress, which needs a
   second egress, which needs Dan. Criterion 2 names an unanswered verdict as legitimate; this
   records it as a **decision with a reason**, not as a gap. **What the window DOES narrow** is
   recorded beside it: Amazon reading from this egress, this fingerprint and this request pattern
   on 2026-09-14 is evidence that *this egress* is not uniformly burned, which is a smaller claim
   than an answer and is worth exactly what it is worth.

## What this plan must NOT do

- **Not compute a duty cycle for Amazon from one reset.** One reset in one window has no
  denominator. Phase 9's day test was repaired three days ago for precisely that error and
  repeating it here would be the same mistake with a different subject. Record the observations and
  the window; do not divide them.
- **Not edit any earlier verdict away.** § Amazon currently reads `**Verdict: REACHABLE (rung 1)**`
  and § Target `**Verdict: REACHABLE (rung 3)**`. If a verdict line moves, the original and the
  date it was true stay beside it — `docs/retailer-evidence.md` § 6's rule.
- **Not touch `README.md`** — that is `11-02`'s, and its gate is two-directional, so splitting the
  edit across two plans would leave the tree red between them.

## How this plan is watched going red

`tests/test_evidence_check.py` already gates the shape of a verdict. The new content is prose, and
prose is gated by `scripts/evidence_check.py --phase` and by `scripts/identity_check.py --all`.
**Both must be run, and the identity checker is the one that has fired on this project's own prose
four times** — a journal excerpt quoting a host is exactly its subject, so the extracted series must
be checked before it is pasted.
