<objective>
Make the store change the verdict, and stop the alerts naming causes nobody measured.

Two halves, deliberately in one plan. The first turns 05-01's recorded fact into a
refusal to answer: an unpinned or unexpected store is `Availability.UNKNOWN` before any
stock verdict can form, and both guards are watched going red. The second withdraws the
two sentences REQ-15 exists because of — *"we are asking too often"* and *"the detector is
probably broken"* — plus the hardcoded `"bot-y: detector problem"` title that says the same
thing on the one surface a phone actually shows.

**Why one plan and not two.** Both halves rewrite `monitor.assess_health`: the store gap
adds an arm, and REQ-15 rewrites the two arms already there. Two plans inside one function
is contention disguised as parallelism.

**Purpose.** On 2026-08-09 the daemon recorded the Walmart milk control `OUT_OF_STOCK` at
$3.17 while three live reads minutes later returned `IN_STOCK` at $2.42. A parser bug does
not change a price — two different stores answered, and the system published one store's
shelf as a fact about another's. 05-01 made the store recordable. This plan makes a reading
from the wrong store stop being a verdict at all. And on 2026-08-04 every failing control
was reported as a broken detector while the detectors were fine, which cost 20 pages in 24
hours; the sentence that did it is still in `monitor.py`, and so is the rate diagnosis that
kept firing after a 6-hour backoff had been **observed not to help**.

**Output.** `models.STORE_SCOPED`; two guards in `_verdict_from_html`; a fourth
`assess_health` arm; three rewritten alert strings and one rewritten title; one shared
`CAUSE_UNKNOWN` constant; mutations M9 and M10; and `tests/test_alert_text.py`, the gate for
a claim about absence.

**NOT in this plan.**
- *Nothing about `Pacer` persistence.* Criteria 5 and 6 are 05-03's, and `boty/cli.py`,
  `boty/pacing.py` and `boty/config.py` are 05-03's files. Do not open them. This plan does
  enter `tests/test_pacing.py`, for three assertions and a docstring line and nothing else —
  see Task 2, where the entry is argued.
- *No new notification sender.* The store gap rides `send_health_warning`, which is already
  wired into `watch_cycle`'s delivery rollback. Adding a second sender would need
  `boty/cli.py`, which this plan does not own, and an unwired send is "not a retry — it is a
  drop nothing will ever mention again" (`cli.py:244-249`).
- *No re-doing 05-01.* `Watch.store_id`, `Result.store` and `parse.nextdata_store` exist and
  are correct. Take their names and semantics exactly as 05-01 shipped them. In particular:
  `Result.store` is `str | None`, `None` means *the page did not say which store answered*,
  there is no `"0"` special case anywhere, and `"0"` in the fixtures is this repo's own
  redaction placeholder from `8dec2e0`, not a Walmart sentinel.
- *No ROADMAP or STATE edits.* 05-04 closes the phase.

**Expected and correct after this plan: both Walmart watches on this host read UNKNOWN.**
`config/products.yaml` ships `store_id: ${WALMART_STORE_ID}` and that variable is unset here
until 05-04's checkpoint, so the config-gap guard fires and `boty check` shows Walmart
unhealthy with the store-gap reason. That is criterion 2 working, not a regression. Do not
"fix" it by inventing a default.
</objective>
