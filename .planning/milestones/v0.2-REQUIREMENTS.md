### Milestone v0.2 — Say Only What You Measured

<!-- Every one of these closes a claim the system was making without having measured it.
     Scoped 2026-08-10 from four days of live operation after Phase 4. v1.0.0 stays open
     and untagged; none of these depend on it and none of them close it. -->

- [x] **REQ-14**: A Walmart reading states which store it came from, and a reading from an unpinned or unexpected store is UNKNOWN rather than a verdict. **DECIDED 2026-08-10 (Dan): required config, no default.** A `store_id` (or equivalent) is set per Walmart watch in `config/products.yaml`; with no value set, readings are UNKNOWN and the health message says why. Rejected: defaulting to whatever Walmart assigns and only flagging *changes* — it leaves a reading as a statement about an arbitrary store, which is the bug. Rejected: geolocating from a postal code — more moving parts, and the ZIP is precisely the value Phase 3.1's leak incident was about. **bot-y never guesses where the user lives, and a missing pin can never masquerade as a verdict.** Costs every user one setup step, accepted deliberately. Measured 2026-08-09: the same URL returned `OUT_OF_STOCK`/$3.17 to the daemon and `IN_STOCK`/$2.42 to three consecutive live reads minutes later. The differing **price** is what proves the mechanism — a parser bug does not change a price; two different stores answered. Applies to the GO Plus + product watch, not only the control.
- [x] **REQ-15**: No alert names a cause that was not measured. The two live counterexamples are the whole requirement: *"the detector is probably broken"* fired while the detector demonstrably worked, and *"we are asking too often"* kept firing after backing off to a 6-hour interval had been observed not to help. Where the cause is unknown, the alert says so.
- [x] **REQ-16**: A notification is sent only when a human decision changes the outcome. A refusal the backoff is actively handling is recorded, not pushed; a refusal that outlasts the cap is pushed once; a detector producing a *wrong* verdict is pushed immediately. Recording and notifying stay separate — a retailer that is not being watched right now is real information even when there is no action to take.
- [x] **REQ-17**: The price ceiling applies to the **delivered total**, not the item price, and a shipping cost that cannot be resolved produces UNKNOWN rather than a pass. A $54.99 listing with $45 shipping currently defeats one of only two defences against a reseller alert.
  **REVISED BY DAN, 2026-08-11 — the sentence above is the original and is deliberately
  left unedited.** Phase 3.1's format: the reversal is recorded beside the requirement,
  never over it, because a *user* reversing a decision and an *agent* rewording a criterion
  so finished work looks successful are different acts and this project keeps catching
  itself at the second one. His words, verbatim: *"I think where we don't know just send
  it. If the user gets there and it's 50 dollar shipping that's disappointing but it's
  worse to feel like you 'missed out'."* And on the format, also his: *"Instead of
  'unverified', why don't you say price: &lt;price&gt; shipping: &lt;unknown&gt;"*.
  **What is reversed:** the second half of the first sentence. An unresolvable shipping
  cost no longer suppresses the alert — the ceiling falls back to the **item price** and
  the push goes out reading `price: $54.99   shipping: unknown`, with no delivered total
  stated in either branch. **The hole the second sentence names is therefore REOPENED,
  knowingly, by the user**, and a visible empty field is the whole of the mitigation.
  **What still stands:** the first half entire — the ceiling measures the delivered total
  wherever a shipping cost can be read, and a *resolvable* total above the ceiling is still
  suppressed; nothing is guessed anywhere (no figure parsed out of prose, the Walmart
  delivery-fee field still read nowhere, a negative figure still refused, `None` never
  collapsing to `$0.00`); and this touches `alertable`, never `Availability`. **06-01's
  measurements were all correct and not one was re-taken or softened — only the conclusion
  drawn from them changed, and it was changed by the person the tool pages.** Shipped by
  06-07 (`717015b`…`b9e39bc`), recorded by 06-06.
- [x] **REQ-18**: Every claim in the README support matrix is bound to the code it describes. Today the **Rung** cell is not: mutating `check_amazon` to return `Rung.BROWSER`, directly contradicting the shipped `| Amazon | 1 | dom |` row, left 131 tests green. Routing and Extraction are already pinned; Rung is the gap.
  **Two figures in the sentence above are stale and BOTH ARE FLAGGED RATHER THAN EDITED**,

## REQ-17's revision — Dan, 2026-08-11

**REQ-17 is complete _as revised_, and the revision reopens the exact hole the requirement
was written to close.** It is recorded here in Phase 3.1's house format: the original quoted
intact, the reversal beside it, never over it. That form exists because **a user reversing a
decision and an agent rewording a criterion so finished work looks successful are different
acts**, and this project keeps catching itself at the second one.

**The original, unedited** (it is still above, in `## Requirements, as written`, and it is
byte-identical to the pre-phase baseline `4994c7d`):

> **REQ-17**: The price ceiling applies to the **delivered total**, not the item price, and a
> shipping cost that cannot be resolved produces UNKNOWN rather than a pass. A $54.99 listing
> with $45 shipping currently defeats one of only two defences against a reseller alert.

**Dan, 2026-08-11, verbatim:**

> *"I think where we don't know just send it. If the user gets there and it's 50 dollar
> shipping that's disappointing but it's worse to feel like you 'missed out'."*

And on the alert format, also his:

> *"Instead of 'unverified', why don't you say price: &lt;price&gt; shipping: &lt;unknown&gt;"*

**What is reversed:** the second half of the first sentence. An unresolvable shipping cost no
longer suppresses the alert — the ceiling falls back to the **item price** and the push goes
out reading `price: $54.99   shipping: unknown`, with no delivered total stated in either
branch. **The hole the second sentence names is therefore REOPENED, knowingly, by the user**,
and a visible empty field is the whole of the mitigation.

**What still stands:** the first half entire — the ceiling measures the delivered total
wherever a shipping cost can be read, and a *resolvable* total above the ceiling is still
suppressed; nothing is guessed anywhere (no figure parsed out of prose, Walmart's real `7.95`
`fulfillmentPrice` still read nowhere because it is a from-store delivery fee, a negative
figure still refused, `None` never collapsing to `$0.00`); and this touches `alertable`, never
`Availability`.

**The measured cost, which is why this is a trade and not a slip.** Under 06-01's strict rule,
against the built tree, **two of four GO Plus + product watches stopped being able to page** —
Nintendo (the only entry in `config/products.yaml` listing the product at its **$54.99 MSRP
with no marketplace attached**, so the honest rule silenced the watch most likely to be a
genuine restock) and Amazon — with Walmart honestly recorded as *"not demonstrated"*. Under
the reversal **all four can alert again**, Walmart moving off *not demonstrated* on the **same
unchanged measurement**. **06-01's measurements were all correct and not one was re-taken,
softened or re-interpreted — only the conclusion drawn from them changed, and it was changed
by the person the tool pages, after he was shown the bill.**

**Where it is recorded:** REQUIREMENTS.md (above), ROADMAP.md's Phase 6 criterion-1 cell,
`STATE.md`, `docs/retailer-evidence.md` § *Phase 6 closing record*, `06-VERIFICATION.md` as a
signed override (`accepted_by: dan`), `README.md` in plain words to a stranger — *"a listing
with large unread shipping can reach you"* — and as comments beside the branches that
implement it in `boty/models.py` and `boty/notify.py`. **`CHANGELOG.md` was the seventh place
and it was missed**; the milestone audit found it, and it was closed at `0d6d1b8`.

**Phase 6 criterion 1 is recorded MET IN PART**, not rounded up to MET. Shipped by 06-07
(`717015b`…`b9e39bc`), applied to the record by 06-06.

### Definition of Done

v1.0 ships when **both** are true:

1. Five or more retailers report stock with all control products green (of ~7 targeted: GameStop, Walmart, Best Buy, Pokémon Center, Nintendo, Target, Amazon). *Standing at four after Phase 3; Phase 3.1 adds Target and Amazon, which clears this.*
2. Dan has successfully bought a Pokémon GO Plus +

The second is not a joke requirement. The tool exists to solve one concrete
problem, and a monitor that runs beautifully while the thing stays unbought
has not worked.

### User Stories

- **As Dan**, I want a push notification the moment a GO Plus + is buyable *from a retailer, at near MSRP*, so I can buy it before it sells out — and not be woken up by a reseller listing at $229.
- **As Dan**, I want to be told when a detector stops working, so a silent parser failure doesn't cost me the drop while the dashboard looks healthy.
- **As a contributor**, I want to add a retailer by writing one adapter plus a control product, and have tests tell me if I broke anything.

### Non-Functional Requirements

- **Trustworthiness over coverage.** Where they conflict, correctness wins. Ten provably-correct retailers beat a hundred maybes. This is the tiebreaker for every scoping decision.
- **No-browser-first.** A browser is a last resort. It is slower, heavier, and empirically less effective against these targets than TLS impersonation.
- **Polite polling.** 5-minute default with jitter. Never sub-minute.
- **Secrets never in the repo.** Credentials live in a mode-600 env file loaded by systemd, set through a tool that prompts hidden and verifies before writing.
- **Small dependency surface.** Every dependency is another thing that can silently break a monitor.
- **Works from a fresh clone.** A retailer's PRIMARY path must work for someone who clones the repo and adds no credentials. Paths requiring a credential most people cannot obtain — manual approval, a paid domain, a commercial agreement — may be supported as an OPTIONAL enhancement, but never as the documented way that retailer works. A capability the average user cannot enable is a footnote, not support.

### Acceptance Criteria

- `boty check` shows ≥5 retailers, every control in stock, no health warnings
- `make verify` exits 0 on a healthy tree
- Deliberately breaking an extractor makes `make verify` exit non-zero
- A retailer with no control watch is surfaced as unhealthy
- A marketplace listing above the price ceiling does not produce an alert
- A blocked or unparseable fetch produces UNKNOWN, never OUT_OF_STOCK
- Telegram delivery verified end to end via `boty-ping`
